Talk Trial

Privacy Policy

Talk Trial is a private speaking practice app. It helps you rehearse interviews, presentations, and difficult conversations without an account system or developer-operated backend.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

No account

Talk Trial does not ask you to create an account or submit a name, email address, or phone number.

Local practice

Practice history and transcripts are stored on your device using iOS local storage.

No tracking

The app does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking tools.

Information the app uses

Talk Trial uses your microphone when you start a practice session. The app uses Apple speech recognition technology to create a transcript so it can estimate pace, filler words, and speaking patterns. When on-device recognition is available, the app requests on-device recognition. If on-device recognition is not available, Apple speech recognition services may process audio to provide transcription.

Talk Trial may store practice transcripts, session dates, durations, scores, app appearance preference, onboarding status, and reminder preference locally on your device.

Information we do not collect

We do not operate a backend server for Talk Trial. We do not collect your practice audio, transcripts, scores, contacts, location, photos, or personal identifiers on our own servers. We do not sell personal data.

Purchases

Talk Trial Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card information or Apple Account credentials. Apple may provide subscription status information to the app so premium access can be unlocked.

Data storage and deletion

Practice history is stored on your device. You can delete your local practice data from inside the app where reset controls are available, or by deleting the app from your device. Because we do not run a Talk Trial account server, deleting local app data is the primary way to remove stored practice history.

Children

Talk Trial is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact the developer through the App Store support link.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. If future versions add an account system, cloud sync, analytics, or backend services, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Support

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact and support options listed on Talk Trial's App Store product page.
